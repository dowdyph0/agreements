# Privacy Statement

## 1. Introduction

This statement provides a simple overview of how information related to the use of this service is handled. Our goal is to treat information responsibly, transparently, and only when it is needed to provide and improve the user experience.

## 2. Information We May Collect

While using the service, certain information may be collected as part of its normal operation. This may include information provided directly by the user, information related to how the service is used, and basic technical information about the device or environment used to access it.

The type and amount of information collected may vary depending on the features being used.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Collected information may be used to operate and maintain the service, provide its features, identify technical issues, understand how the service is being used, and make general improvements.

The intention is not to use collected information for purposes unrelated to the operation or improvement of the service.

## 4. Information Retention

Information is generally kept for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes for which it was collected. When information is no longer needed, it may be deleted or no longer used.

## 5. Information Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information from unauthorized access, modification, or use. However, no system used to store or transmit information can be considered completely free from risk.

## 6. Sharing of Information

Personal information is not shared indiscriminately. When external services are required to provide certain features, only the information reasonably necessary for those features may be shared.

## 7. Changes to This Statement

This statement may be updated when the service or the way information is handled changes. The most recent version will be made available alongside the service.

## 8. Contact

Questions or concerns regarding this statement or the handling of information can be directed through the usual contact channel available for the service.
